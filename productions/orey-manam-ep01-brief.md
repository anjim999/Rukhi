# PROJECT: OREY… MANAM!
## EPISODE 01 — BIRTHDAY EMERGENCY

You are the director, cinematographer, character-animation supervisor, dialogue director, and continuity supervisor for a premium-quality realistic 3D animated Indian youth comedy series.

Create Episode 1 from the supplied screenplay. Preserve the original story, dialogue, comedic timing, character personalities, and ending.

The episode introduces six close friends through an ordinary WhatsApp group-chat emergency that turns out to be Karthik announcing his birthday. The scene should feel authentic, cinematic, relatable, and funny to Indian youth.

### 1. VISUAL STYLE

Create a cinematic 3D animated production with realistic human proportions, natural Indian facial features, detailed hair, realistic skin shading, physically believable lighting, natural cloth simulation, convincing facial expressions, and realistic movement.

The visual quality should resemble a premium animated streaming series for young adults.

Do not create a children's cartoon, anime, exaggerated caricature, mobile-game aesthetic, or a slideshow of still images.

Use realistic Indian bedrooms, offices, workspaces, food, smartphones, furniture, and contemporary clothing.

Aspect ratio: 16:9.
Frame rate: 24 fps.
Resolution: 1920 × 1080 minimum; use 4K where supported.
Visual continuity: cinematic, natural, consistent, and grounded.

### 3. CHARACTER CONSISTENCY RULES

Before generating cinematic footage, produce a permanent character reference sheet for each of the six characters.

Reference sheets must include front-facing, three-quarter, profile, full-body, and facial-expression views.

Attach or reuse the correct reference images whenever generating a shot.

If the tool supports character IDs, saved character models, reference conditioning, persistent seeds, or identity locking, use those features.

Never use a different character reference for the same person merely because the camera angle changes.

Changing expressions is allowed. Changing identity is not.

Preserve facial bone structure, eyes, nose, lips, skin tone, hairline, hairstyle, body proportions, age appearance, and established wardrobe.
